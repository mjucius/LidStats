LidStats

This package installs the menu bar app and a privileged helper that changes macOS sleep settings during a keep-awake session.

Closed-lid use is at your own risk. Keeping a MacBook awake with its lid closed can make it easier to overheat. Leave the lid open when practical and keep the Mac ventilated. Never put an awake Mac in a bag or other enclosed space.

LidStats attempts to switch to Low Power Mode and end the session when macOS reports critical heat. These checks do not guarantee cooling, sleep, shutdown, or protection from damage. Damage caused by use outside Apple’s published operating guidelines may be excluded from warranty coverage.
